After installation

  1. The app opens by itself. Its icon is a green ö in the menu bar (top right of the screen). It does not appear in the Dock.
  2. macOS asks for two permissions: in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and Input Monitoring, turn on “Yangi Alifbo”. Without them macOS does not let keyboard apps work.
  3. Once allowed, the app reopens itself once and starts working (if macOS says “Quit & Reopen”, click it).

Controls

  • Control+Option+U — turn on / off (for example, while typing in English).
  • Backspace right after a replacement restores the original spelling (ş → sh).
  • Typing s'h does not produce ş: Is'hoq → Ishoq.
  • Menu bar icon → Settings… — language, app list, statistics, start at login and more.
