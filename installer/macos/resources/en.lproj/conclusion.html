Installed!

Yangi Alifbo is running. Look for the green ö icon in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

If macOS asks for permission: in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and Input Monitoring, turn on “Yangi Alifbo”.

If permission is requested again and again (a leftover entry from an older version): remove “Yangi Alifbo” from the list with − and open the app again.

You can change the app language (Uzbek, new alphabet, Russian, English) in Settings → General → Language.

You can now close the installer.
