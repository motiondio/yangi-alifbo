Privacy

The app watches and replaces typed letters only on your computer. Typed text is never saved anywhere or sent to anyone. Statistics (how many letters were fixed — numbers only) are also stored only on your computer.

Once a day the app asks GitHub for the latest version number, and when Settings is opened it downloads the list of links and announcements (promo.json). Your text and personal data are never sent.
